# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, criticise ideas and code rather than people, and keep the project a place where anyone can take part regardless of experience, background or identity. Harassment, insults and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable.

It applies in the repositories, issues, pull requests and any other space of the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, write to the maintainers through the contact form at https://domia.ai/contact. Reports are handled privately and the reporter's identity is not shared.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits and contributions that break this code, and may block someone who keeps doing so.
